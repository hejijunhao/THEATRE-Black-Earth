# Combat paper — estimate / AAR

Language to steal, not invent. Anchors: **HOI4 land-battle / combat-prediction window**, **Vic 3 battle estimate and result**, and **EU5 only as a reject** (pip-dice theater).

Screenshots of those live panels are **not in this folder**. This environment cannot boot HOI4 / Vic 3 / EU5. Do not invent replacement chrome while the shots are missing — strip decorative dice and keep the numbers Paradox already uses.

## Steal (HOI4)

The land-battle window is two named sides, a predicted or current **trend**, and **losses as counts**. Wiki language (`Land battle`): attacker vs defender, org / HP damage, manpower and equipment casualties, terrain and other modifiers as a list. The battle icon is a **forecast number** (progress toward holding or breaking) plus a green / yellow / red trend — not a die face.

After the fight the same window resolves to: who held, who retreated, casualty totals. No pip grid.

Steal for us:

- **Odds / trend as the middle number.** Ratio or advantage label between the two names.
- **Strength as a bill.** Before → after, or a loss delta. HOI4’s HP / manpower row is the cousin of our StrengthStrip.
- **One-line outcome.** Held / fell back / destroyed. That is the verdict, not a roll call.
- **Confirm and cancel.** Issue the attack or stand down. The paper does not ask the player to watch dice.

## Steal (Vic 3)

Battle estimate: troop counts, predicted casualties, morale. Battle result: losses on each side and who kept the ground. Still numbers. Still no faces.

Steal the thinness: estimate and result are the **same sheet family**. Predicted exchange on the way in; realized delta on the way out.

## Reject (EU4 / EU5)

EU land combat *is* a pip-dice UI: die faces, roll totals, and a fortune strip that competes with the odds. That is the pattern Phil named as not useful. Do not re-home the 2d6 theater as a second row of totals or ×fortune badges. The simulation may still swing; the paper does not illustrate the swing as dice.

## BLACK THEATRE target

Staff estimate and after-action stay one CombatPaper family (wash, not curtain).

Keep, in this order of rank:

1. One-line verdict (estimate: staff advantage; AAR: held / fell back / destroyed).
2. Odds in the middle.
3. Strength before → after (and the −delta when something was lost).
4. One confirm plate and one dismiss plate on the estimate; one dismiss on the AAR.

Drop: die-face glyphs, pip grids, “2d6” as chrome, “before dice” / “the roll” as the confirm language, the AAR fortune row (faces + totals + ×fortune).

Honest gap vs HOI4: we do not have a live 0–100 battle-progress bar or hourly org ticks. This is a single-resolution PC2 exchange presented as staff paper. Do not fake a HOI4 progress pip to replace the dice we removed.
