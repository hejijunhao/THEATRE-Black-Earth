# Combat paper stamps — steal / reject

Decorative **ASSAULT / DISPATCH / FIRES** classification on the staff
sheet. Anchors: **Vic 3 thin header strip** and **HOI4 land-battle
window type**. Combat-paper numbers (odds, strength, verdict) stay
locked from 0.2.24. Live Paradox screenshots are still TODO in this
folder — do not invent a rubber-badge theater or a second ledger to
fake them.

## Steal (Vic 3 primary)

The play header is a **thin continuous strip**: identity left, short
marks right, map still owns the frame. Steal that for the paper:

- Classification and kicker share **one rail**
- Type, not a boxed object
- No rotation, fill, or drop shadow competing with the headline

## Steal (HOI4)

The land-battle window names the act (attack / battle / result) as
header chrome, then the **numbers**. The mark is a file class, not a
wax seal.

Steal: Assault on the estimate, Dispatch on the after-action, Fires
when the battery is the act. One word.

## Reject

- Rotated rubber-stamp badge (border + fill + inset glow + 7° tilt)
- A second ledger row of chips on the strip
- EU pip-dice or fortune chrome riding the mark
- Armor / INF / MECH / ARTY retouch; ground; reach; atmosphere;
  Vic political flat colour

## BLACK THEATRE target

CombatPaper mounts a `.stamp-strip`: kicker left (`Staff estimate ·
week N` / `After action · week N`), mark right (`Assault` /
`Dispatch` / `Fires`). Hairline only. Headline owns the sheet. Event
`Decision` stays on the boxed mark — that is not this slice.

Vic 3's shipped play header and HOI4's land-battle window type, not a
reconstruction of their art.

**What they do, and what we copied:**
- Thin classification rail. Ours is ~22px on the sheet, kicker + one
  word. The map stays the hero; the paper does not grow a banner.

  (or Fires). Confirm plates still say Commit / Continue.

**What we did not copy:**
- Vic 3's long resource chip row on this rail (those live on the play
  header, locked 0.2.25).

- A product wordmark or a studio seal.

the strip; dice theater; armor / ground / reach / atmosphere.
